# Proofread wiki pages

Read each page named in the arguments in full. Review without editing. For each page:

- State the page's main claim in your own words based on the body, not just the callout.
- Compare that claim with the bulleted key ideas at the top and, when supplied, the user's intent. Identify any missing callout or key idea the body leaves unsupported.
- Identify any passage that fails to support the stated key ideas. Quote it or give its location, explain the mismatch, and say what needs to change. If there are no such passages, say so.

Give a brief rationale, then end the turn with a single lowercase verdict on its own line: `pass` if no reviewed page requires substantive changes, `fail` otherwise. Optional wording preferences are not grounds for failure.
